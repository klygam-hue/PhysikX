## What this protects, and what it does not

The license restricts authorized reuse of rights actually held by the owner.
It is not copy protection and cannot physically prevent copying or guarantee
recovery against an infringer. Copyright protection generally concerns protected
expression; it does not give a monopoly over physics ideas or mathematical methods.
Existing statutory exceptions and separately licensed third-party rights remain.

For the strongest access control, keep the source repository **private** and
share it only with trusted recipients under appropriate terms. Do not change
repository visibility without the owner's instruction. A public GitHub repository
permits viewing and in-service forks under GitHub's Terms of Service even with
restrictive licensing. Making it private later does not erase existing copies.

a license file rather than MIT. The .NET package points to the root LICENSE.
Source files carry the custom license identifier. Binaries are covered by the
